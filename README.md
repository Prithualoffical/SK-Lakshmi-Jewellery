# S.K. Lakshmi Gold Jewellery - Modern Luxury Landing Page

A responsive, high-converting luxury landing page custom-built for **S.K. Lakshmi Gold Jewellery** (Dharmapuri, Tamil Nadu).

## 🌟 Key Features Built-In

1. **Live Gold & Silver Rates Ticker**:
   - Header ticker displaying 22K Gold, 24K Gold, and Silver per gram.
   - Interactive **Live Gold & Sovereign Price Calculator** supporting grams and Pavans (1 Pavan = 8 grams) with instant rupee conversion.
2. **Curated Collections Showcase**:
   - Filterable categories: *All Jewellery*, *Bridal Harams*, *Antique & Temple*, *Bangles & Kadas*, *Daily Wear & Chains*, and *Pure Silver Articles*.
   - Each product card comes with gram estimation, BIS 916 tag, and direct WhatsApp inquiry with item code.
3. **Monthly Gold Savings Scheme (Thanga Magal) Interactive Planner**:
   - Dynamic monthly installment calculator (₹1,000 to ₹25,000/mo for 11 months).
   - Real-time gold equivalent accumulation display and one-tap WhatsApp enrollment.
4. **Bridal Heritage Spotlight**:
   - Highlights South Indian Muhurtham sets (Kasu Mala, Oddiyanam, Vanki, Jhumkas).
   - Old gold exchange valuation guarantee with 0% hidden deduction.
5. **Showroom Location & Contact (Dharmapuri)**:
   - Full address: No. 26-A, Mohamad Ali Club Road, Dharmapuri - 636701.
   - Direct tap-to-call links (`+91 94423 22081` & `+91 97909 06732`).
   - Interactive showroom visit booking modal & contact form syncing directly to WhatsApp.
6. **WhatsApp Floating Action Button**:
   - Persistent WhatsApp chat trigger formatted with friendly greeting.
